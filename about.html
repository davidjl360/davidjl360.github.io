About Me

Enterprise cloud architecture and technical leadership, grounded in 20 years of hands-on depth

My Story

I'm a Staff Infrastructure Architect at GE HealthCare with 20 years of experience across cloud, networking, security, and operations. Over my career I've grown from Senior Engineer to Principal Engineer to my current architect role, building deep technical foundations along the way.

My work doesn't read like someone whose primary value is operating AWS or Azure. I get brought into ambiguous, cross-domain problems where infrastructure, networking, security, operations, and organizational ownership collide. The recurring pattern is consistent: architect, investigate, challenge assumptions, establish evidence, identify risk, recommend a path, coordinate the right teams, and drive toward resolution.

I operate across engineering, CloudOps, security, SRE, networking, application teams, vendors, and leadership, translating highly technical operational detail into leadership-level priorities. Underneath that sits strong technical depth in AWS, Azure, security, and networking.
